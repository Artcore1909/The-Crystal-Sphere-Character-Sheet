1. Core Mechanics & Dice Rolling ▾
The Core Mechanic (The Roll):
  1. Roll up to six dice determined by the Governing Aspect, skills, and racial abilities.
  2. Modify the dice pool as allowed by your skills and other active effects.
  3. Count Hits as 1 and Crits as your Crit Count (default is 2).
  4. Multiply by the number of Crit Results above zero (applies only to Attacks and Avoidance).
  5. Add your Bonus (for Attacks and Avoidance, only if the attack hits).
  6. Compare your final total to the Target Number (TN): you must meet or beat the TN to succeed.

Skill Checks, Hidden Checks, & Confrontations:
  • Skill Check: Compare your total roll to the TN set by the LM. The difference represents your degree of success or failure.
  • Hidden Checks: May be rolled secretly by the LM in your stead (such as Smuggling or Thievery) to preserve character ignorance.
  • Skill Confrontations: Occur when the TN is replaced by the target's active skill check. In the case of a tie, the aggressor (initiator) fails. All Observation skills are considered aggressive.
  • Assisted Checks: When assisting an ally, add your bonus directly to their check.

Proficiency & Untrained Actions:
  • Untrained: You may attempt non-cast skills without training; however, untrained rolls cannot be modified and are made at disadvantage.
  • Proficient: You may change up to your Rank in Skill Results to Hit Results whenever rolling for a proficient skill.
  • Extra Training: You may reroll Blank Results once whenever rolling for a proficient skill.
  • Mastery: Add one Crit Result whenever rolling for a proficient skill.
2. Aspects & Secondary Attributes ▾
Primary Aspects:
  • Dexterity (Dex): Governs physical reaction, movement, Dexterity saves, Avoidance, and light weapon attacks.
  • Health (Hel): Governs physical fortitude, Health saves, and Vitality regeneration. Added thrice to your Vitality pool.
  • Strength (Str): Governs physical power, Strength saves, jumping/climbing, and heavy weapon attacks. Added twice to your Vitality pool.
  • Acuity (Act): Governs focus, awareness, Acuity saves, Observation, and Mana regeneration. Added thrice to your Mana pool.
  • Intellect (Int): Governs learning, retention, Intellect saves, and knowledge/lore skills. Prerequisite for Cast Skills.
  • Verve (Ver): Governs social influence, magical manipulation, Verve saves, and cast skills. Added twice to your Mana pool.
  • Hap: Governs raw luck. Your Luck pool is equal to Hap.

Secondary Stats (Formulas):
  • Vitality Pool: $(3 \times \text{Health}) + (2 \times \text{Strength})$
  • Mana Pool: $(3 \times \text{Acuity}) + (2 \times \text{Verve})$
  • Luck Pool: Equal to Hap. Completely regenerates at the end of each session.
  • Initiative: Roll a dice pool equal to $\text{Dexterity} + \text{Acuity}$ (Max 6d; excess dice add flat +1 each).
  • Athletic Paces: Default balance, climb, jump, and swim paces are a quarter of your move pace rounded down.

Regeneration & Rest:
  • For each 2 phases of rest, roll dice equal to your Health (for Vitality) and Acuity (for Mana). Regenerate 1 point per Hit and 2 points per Crit. Resting all 3 phases allows rolling twice.
3. Character Creation, Races & Cultures ▾
Character Creation:
  • All characters begin with 108 Exp. to invest in Aspects and Skills. At creation, starting Aspects are set to 3 plus an unmodified 2-dice roll (Hits are 1, Crits are 2), except Hap which is determined by rolling 4 dice (rerolling Blank, Skill, and Wyrd once).

Sex Adjustments:
  • Males: Gain a +1 to assign to Health, Strength, or Verve.
  • Females: Gain a +1 to assign to Dexterity, Acuity, or Verve.

Racial Pace & Customizations:
  • Races determine move pace, starting modifiers, and special abilities (such as Dwarven Earthray Vision or Elven Inexhaustible Mana).

Culture & Language Fluency:
  • Characters speak their native language and all included pidgins. Having an Intellect greater than 2 allows upgrading one pidgin to full fluency.
4. Combat & Action Economy ▾
Turn Structure:
  • Initiative: Roll at the start of combat. High rolls act first.
  • Simultaneous Actions: When sharing initiative, combatants resolve actions fully, though incantations/full actions can be interrupted.
  • Right of Firststrike: When your melee range is breached, you can attack the approaching combatant immediately if you are unengaged and ungrappled.

The Pulse:
  • Combat is resolved in pulses. Each pulse, combatants complete or initiate actions:
  • Words, Free Actions, and Standard Actions resolve in the pulse they are declared.
  • Move Actions and Incantations are initiated in one pulse and complete on the following pulse.

Types of Actions:
  • Attack: Move up to 1 hex and make an attack. Hits if your attack roll exceeds the target's Avoidance roll.
  • Move: Move hexes up to your pace. Resolved on the following pulse.
  • Cast, Charge, and Skill Checks: Actions that consume part or all of a pulse.
  • Hold: Delay your action to act on a later pulse or initiative.
5. Combat Conditions Index ▾
Combat Conditions:

Conditions limit a character's ability to act. Unless specified, the save TN is 2. Re-inflicting an active condition increases its save TN by 1.

  • Actively Dying: Must pass a TN 0 Health (Vitality loss/Asphyxiation) or Acuity (Mana loss) save each Pulse or die. Stabilize with healing, TN 3 Street Smarts/Survival, or the Stabilize spell.
  • Advantage: Convert all Hit results into Crits (occurs against prone/sleeping/immobile targets).
  • Blind: Attacks and Avoidance rolls are made at disadvantage. Blind Dexterity saves automatically fail. Recover sight with a Health save each Pulse.
  • Bound & Gagged/Entangled: Cannot attack, and Avoidance is at disadvantage. Free speech/casting is allowed unless gagged. Escape with a Strength save or Escape check.
  • Confused: Cannot tell friend from foe. Movement and targets are chosen randomly. Recover with an Acuity save each Pulse.
  • Dazed: Cannot take actions or modify Avoidance rolls. Recover with an Acuity save each Pulse.
  • Disadvantaged: Remove all Hit results from your roll.
  • Envenomed/Poisoned: Cannot receive healing until neutralized. Save Health each time you would suffer damage to fight off.
  • Fatigued/Exhausted: Cumulative -1d penalty to all rolls. Recover exertion with 1 pulse of rest per fatigue level.
  • Grappled/Pinned: Grappled targets are in contact. The controller can make one-handed attacks or drag the target. Escape with an Escape check or grapple contest. Both targets are at disadvantage to avoid outside strikes.
  • Grievously Wounded: Recover only 1 Vitality per day, and only if treated with a TN 2 Survival check.
  • Impaired: Suffer -1d to Dexterity and Strength checks/saves. Cumulative.
  • Nauseated: Must pass a Health save before acting. Rolling a 0 causes vomiting as a Standard Action.
  • Off-balance: No actions until recovered. Regain balance with a Balance check or Dexterity save; failure causes falling. Made at disadvantage.
  • Pained: Must pass a Verve save before taking any action.
  • Panicked: Must flee. Must pass a Hap save (TN 0) or drop held items. Recover with an Acuity save each Pulse.
  • Paralyzed/Petrified: No actions. Attacks against you are made with advantage. Recover with a Verve save each Pulse.
  • Prone & Supine: Attack and avoid at disadvantage (except crossbows). Prone characters cannot shoot arc-bows or use shields. Takes 1 pulse to stand up.
  • Numbed: Move pace and Dexterity are reduced by half. Recover with a Health save each Pulse.
  • Surprised: Fail an Acuity save at start of combat? Unable to act until the following Pulse.
  • Weakened: Strength is halved. Recover with a Health save each Pulse.
6. Equipment, Customization & Sundering ▾
Critical Hits by Damage Type:
  • Bash: Dazes the target.
  • Slash: Impairs the target (-1d to Dex/Str skills and saves).
  • Crush: Grapples or pins the target.
  • Thrust: Gains +1 to its Crit Count (cc).

Armor & Shields:
  • Armor coverage determines Crit Count, and weight determines Damage Reduction (DR). Unarmored Crit Count is 2 by default. Sleeping in armor cuts regeneration in half and inflicts a cumulative -1d penalty to all actions.

Grand Bazaar & Enchantments:
  • Each character starts with £1000. Price Formula: (Base Cost × Special Qualities) + Enchantment = Total Cost.
  • Law of Duplicate Effects: Similar magical effects do not stack; only the stronger effect manifests.

Sundering & Tool Use:
  • Attacking environmental targets (sundering) is processed using the Sundering table. Sunder targets require appropriate tools. Using an improper tool risks sundering the tool itself (requires a Hap save relative to the material: Wood TN 0, Metal TN 1, Stone TN 2).
7. Skills & Magic Systems ▾
Action Types & Time Requirements:
  • Words: Resolve instantly. Cost 6 Mana to cast.
  • Incantations: Resolve 1 Pulse after declaration. Cost 3 Mana. Can be interrupted (requires a save against damage taken).
  • Encænia: Take longer to perform. Practical only outside combat. Cost 1 Mana.
  • Rites: Take one full phase of the day (1/6th of a day). Cost 72 Mana and require a specific focus (usually blood).

Cast Skills (Spells):
  • Spells require Intellect to learn and Mana to cast. Channeling costs 1 Mana per Pulse. Intellect requirements to learn spells scale progressively based on their position in the skill tree (first slot requires 1, second requires 2, eighth requires 8).
  • Unless specified, all spells have a maximum range equal to 6 times the caster's Verve in hexes.

Temple Spells & Sage Skills:
  • Temple spells require a Holy Symbol and do not work against fellow worshippers of the same deity.
  • Sage skills require Intellect. The total number of Sage skills you possess can never exceed your Intellect.
8. Lore & World Atlas ▾
The Creation:
  • The Crystal Sphere is composed of air, earth, water, and magma. Day and Night rotate atop the Crystal Axis.
  • The Four Moons (Deities): Ver (the green moon/Sea Lord creator), Aestas (the red moon/Fire God creator), Autumnus (the orange moon/Earth Goddess creator), and Hiems (the white moon/Wind Goddess creator).
  • Grand Conjunctions: Terrifying alignments occurring once every few thousand years when all four moons are in the sky simultaneously.

The Diaspora & Geography:
  • The Empire: Formed of eight human kingdoms, ruled by an Emperor and a 300-seat senate. Senatorial capital is Bel Agor; military seat is Bel Inaba.
  • The Wildlands: Territory not under imperial control, including the Frosinlands (ruled by Jarls) and Desert of the Jann (theonomic monarchy).
  • Sculpted Darkness: A massive subterranean cavern beneath the mainland, containing diverse topography, minerals, and the Temple of Night.
